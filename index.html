About


Nightmare Newer Wii is a mod based off of Mayro's Nightmare Mario Wii. This project's goal is to insert 128 uniquely horrible levels into Newer Super Mario Bros. Wii. As of right now, there are only 8 worlds completed with the ninth, tenth, eleventh, twelth, thirteenth and fourteenth ones in development. You can watch the development and showcases (spoilers) here. Nightmare Newer Wii began it's development in 2024, just a year after the original Nightmare Mario Wii, it's not recommended to play the older versions from 2024 as they have no creativity. The new ones however do have creativity. I hope you enjoy the wonderful levels I put lots of effort into...
